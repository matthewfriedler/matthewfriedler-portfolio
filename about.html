About me

I’m a Training Specialist at Contentsquare, the digital experience analytics platform.

My job is helping customers get from “we bought this” to “we use this.” I build and run training that teaches teams to find where visitors struggle on their websites and apps, and to turn what they find into changes worth making.

The part I enjoy most is the conversation before the training: working out what a team is actually trying to fix, then showing them the shortest path to it in the product. That’s most of what solutions consultants and presales teams do, and it’s the direction I’m moving in.

Outside work, I build small web apps to learn by doing. I’d rather understand how something works by building a version of it than by reading about it. Two of them are below.
