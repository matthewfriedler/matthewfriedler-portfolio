About me

Training Specialist at Contentsquare. Builder of small web apps on the side.

I’m a Training Specialist at Contentsquare, the digital experience analytics platform.

My job is helping customers get from “we bought this” to “we use this.” I build and run training that teaches teams to find where visitors struggle on their websites and apps, and to turn what they find into changes worth making.

The part I enjoy most is the conversation before the training: working out what a team is actually trying to fix, then finding the shortest path to it in the product.

Outside work, I build small web apps to learn by doing. I’d rather understand how something works by building a version of it than by reading about it. A few of them are below.
